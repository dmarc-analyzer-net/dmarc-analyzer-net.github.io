Valimail is an enterprise-focused platform known for automated enforcement and
strong compliance credentials, sold through an **enterprise motion** —
quote-based pricing and a sales-led process. DMARC Analyzer sits at the opposite
end: **open source and self-hosted**, free to run on unlimited domains, with no
quote, no sales call, and your data on your own infrastructure.

## Side by side

| | DMARC Analyzer | Valimail |
|---|---|---|
| Hosting | Self-hosted | Cloud SaaS |
| Open source | Yes (Apache-2.0) | No |
| Pricing model | Free; unlimited domains | Enterprise, quote-based |
| Onboarding | `docker compose up` | Sales-led |
| Your report data | Stays on your infrastructure | Stored in the vendor's cloud |
| Best fit | SMB → agency → self-hosting enterprise | Large enterprise |

## Where Valimail fits

If you're a large enterprise that wants a fully managed, sales-supported
enforcement service with formal compliance backing, that's Valimail's home turf.

## Where DMARC Analyzer fits

If you want to **start immediately, self-host, and avoid enterprise pricing and
procurement**, DMARC Analyzer gets you the same core DMARC visibility on your own
terms. Follow the [path from monitoring to
enforcement](/guides/from-monitoring-to-enforcement/) at your own pace.
