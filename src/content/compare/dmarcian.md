dmarcian helped create this category — it was co-founded by one of DMARC's
original architects — and it's a capable, well-supported hosted platform, with a
free tier for personal use and paid plans above it. It is vendor-hosted, priced
by **domains and message volume**, and your report data lives in dmarcian's
cloud. DMARC Analyzer is open source and self-hosted: unlimited domains at no
per-domain cost, with reports that never leave your infrastructure.

## Side by side

*dmarcian's plans as published in July 2026; check their pricing page for current
terms.*

| | DMARC Analyzer | dmarcian |
|---|---|---|
| Hosting | Self-hosted | Vendor cloud, with regional instances |
| Open source | Yes (Apache-2.0) | No |
| Free tier | Everything, unlimited domains | Yes — personal use, 2 domains |
| Paid plans | None | From ~$24/month, by domains + volume |
| Your report data | Stays on your infrastructure | Stored in the vendor's cloud |
| Agencies / many clients | Yes, self-hosted multi-tenant | Yes, on paid plans |
| Support | Community | Vendor |

## Where dmarcian fits

If you want a fully managed service with vendor support and don't need to
self-host, dmarcian is a mature, credible option.

## Where DMARC Analyzer fits

If you care about **data sovereignty**, want **no per-domain cost at scale**, or
value **open source you can audit and run yourself**, DMARC Analyzer is built for
exactly that. New to DMARC? Start by
[publishing your first record](/guides/publish-your-first-dmarc-record/) and
[reading the reports](/guides/how-to-read-a-dmarc-aggregate-report/).
